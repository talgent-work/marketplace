# Planning an Intent

## The tree

Interview the Assignee (`interviewing`):

1. **Split or not.** Split when parts need other people or the whole outgrows this Work. A plan may have no children.
2. **Each child**: its one-sentence title, its Assignee, and what it contributes to this Intent.
3. **Relations**: Intents this one depends on, Intents that depend on it, and Intents it belongs with. Read each in full before calling it a dependency or a duplicate.

## Finish

1. Ask the Assignee with `AskUserQuestion` to confirm the plan their replies settled, and the move to In Progress.
2. Record each child and relation (`recording-intents`). Remove relations that no longer hold.
3. Advance to In Progress, citing the same answer.
4. Mail the PM Coordinator the transition. Mail the Intents the plan touches what changed and why.

Done when every child and relation of the confirmed plan exists, and the Intent is In Progress.
