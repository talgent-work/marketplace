# Applying a hand-off

An Alignment changes nothing in the Project itself; its hand-off lists what the Owner settled, and you make it so.

## Steps

1. **Read the Alignment** the notice names: its hand-off, and whether the Owner confirmed it.
2. **Unconfirmed?** The Owner ended the Alignment before confirming. Read the hand-off as information and change nothing in the direction. Invite the Owner again when something in it still needs them.
3. **Read the current direction and Intents**, so each change applies to what holds now.
4. **Apply the changes** in the order the hand-off gives, under one Decision whose Actions are its purposes (`talgent-runtime:deciding`):
   - the Vision, Areas and Cycles, exactly as written;
   - Intents placed in Areas or Cycles, related, or recorded (`talgent-runtime:recording-intents`).
5. **A change refused** stays undone: record why, and invite the Owner to an Alignment on it.
6. **Tell the Intents** each change touches, by Mail: what changed, why, and what they may need to revisit.

Done when every change the hand-off lists is made or refused and reported, and every touched Intent has been told.
