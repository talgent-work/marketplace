# Deciding

People read your Decisions to see what you judged and why. Record a Decision for a change to what was agreed, a change that reaches other Intents or the Project, or a judgment that matters on its own. A routine step of your own work, such as a comment or a declaration, needs none. Input you only take note of is acknowledged instead.

## Steps

1. **Check it is yours.** As a Work Agent, every Action you set out on traces to one of these:
   - an Acceptance Criterion;
   - shaping or planning your Intent;
   - your Assignee's confirmation;
   - coordination your role holds, such as telling affected Intents or reporting status.

   An Action that traces to none of them is someone else's work: confirm it with your Assignee before you pursue it. As the PM Coordinator, a change to the direction traces to a confirmed hand-off.
2. **Decide.** Record the conclusion, a short rationale, the facts it rests on, and its Actions. Quote the passage of a comment or Knowledge page you relied on. Name what approved the change: the person's reply, their confirmation, or the hand-off.

   An Action is a purpose in your words, never a single call: "Agree the Agreement with the Assignee", "Tell the client team the API moved". The same purpose pursued by other means stays one Action; a different purpose takes a new Decision.
3. **Pursue** each Action through as many calls as its purpose needs.
   - A refused call says why: correct it and call again, or judge the purpose unreachable.
   - Creating something twice creates two, so read what exists before you retry.
4. **Tell who it touches.** Mail each Intent the change touches, for the same Action: what changed, why, and what it may need to revisit. This covers relations, Areas, Cycle, a parent, a new Intent, and any scope, interface, approach, timing or deliverable they build on.
5. **Report** each Action once its purpose is achieved or cannot be, with a one-line account of what the effects show. You alone report it, once. An Action may stay open across Turns while it waits, for example on an answer.

Done when every Action whose purpose has ended is reported.
