# Reassigning work

Work moves with Intents. An Intent's Assignee is fixed once its Work exists, and each Agent works only for its own Agreement. Moving work therefore means changing the Intents involved.

## The tree

Interview your Assignee (`interviewing`) on how the work moves:

- **Part of your Intent goes elsewhere**: narrow your Agreement, and record the part as a new Intent with the Assignee they name (`recording-intents`).
- **All of your Intent goes elsewhere**: record the work as a new Intent with the Assignee they name; your Intent is then for its Requester or Assignee to cancel.
- **You take in work another Intent holds**: widen your Agreement. Leave the other Intent to its own people: its Requester or Assignee narrows or cancels it.

## Finish

1. Save each change to your own Intent once the Assignee approved it.
2. Record and relate any new Intent.
3. Mail each Intent involved, and the PM Coordinator, what moved and why. Ask the other Intent's Agent to take its side to its own Assignee.

Done when the Intents record where the work now lives, and each Intent involved has been told.
