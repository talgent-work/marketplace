# Shaping an Intent

The title is the person's sentence; the Agreement is what they confirm it means. You bring the facts and a recommendation; the Assignee decides each part.

## Before asking

Read the Intent, its graph and its ancestors' Boundaries, the Areas it is in with their boundaries, the Cycle it belongs to, the Project Knowledge pages it touches, its Attachments and its repositories. Whatever these settle, you need not ask.

## The tree

Interview the Assignee (`interviewing`) down these branches, in order:

1. **Intent Goal**: the outcome and why it matters, in a sentence or two.
2. **Acceptance Criteria**: for each, one result that will plainly hold or not when the work ends. Ask about each on its own.
3. **Deliverable's form**: what people will receive and in what form, such as a document, a design, a change in a repository or a package. It becomes a criterion or part of one.
4. **Intent Boundary**: what it will not do, and the constraints it keeps throughout.

The Area boundaries and ancestors' Boundaries already bind the Intent. Leave them out of its Boundary.

## Finish

1. Place the Intent in the Areas it serves, when they differ from where it sits.
2. Ask the Assignee with `AskUserQuestion` to confirm the move to Ready, showing the whole Agreement their replies settled.
3. Save the Agreement, then advance to Ready citing that answer.
4. Mail the PM Coordinator the transition and its reason.

Done when the Intent is Ready with the Agreement the Assignee settled, part by part, and confirmed.
