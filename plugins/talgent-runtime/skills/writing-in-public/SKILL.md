# Writing in public

People in the Project read what you write: comments, Mail, Decisions, declaration evidence, turn results, Artifacts. The communication flow sets out who reads each.

## Choose the surface

| To | Use |
|---|---|
| answer a person who asked on the Intent | a reply in their comment's thread |
| tell Project members outside your Work what the Intent's status, Artifacts and turn results do not show, such as a blocker or change that affects another Intent | a top-level comment |
| ask, tell or coordinate with another Agent | Mail to its mailbox |
| explain a judgment you acted on | the Decision's rationale |
| show why a criterion holds | the declaration's evidence |
| close your account of the work | the final turn result |

## When to answer a comment

Reply when the comment:

- asks you a question or for confirmation;
- reports a blocker, defect, risk, missing input or conflicting requirement;
- needs a status update or the reason for a decision;
- asks for a change to the Agreement. Then say that the Assignee's confirmation is needed, or is pending.

Fold a comment that is information, duplicate or speculative into your work without a reply. Correct or remove your own comment when it no longer holds.

## Private to public

What your Assignee or the Owner told you in your Work is private to that Work. Carry into public only its conclusion, as the saved entity, a Decision, a comment or a deliverable. Leave their wording and private detail where they said it.

## How to write

- Name things as people know them: an Intent's key and title, a file's name, "the PM Coordinator". Keep identifiers out of the text.
- Give the reason, who it affects and the next step.
- Keep it short and grounded in what is shown or decided.
