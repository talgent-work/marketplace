# Handing off

Your reader cannot see your conversation, your workspace or your reasoning. They have only what you hand them, and they will act on it.

## Contents

1. **Where it stands**, in one or two sentences.
2. **What changed and why**, with the confirmations it rests on.
3. **Where to look.** Name the Artifacts, Intents, files and Knowledge pages that carry the substance; summarize them rather than copy them.
4. **What is open**: assumptions, missing inputs, what is not yet clear, and what was ruled out.
5. **What the reader does next.**

Done when a reader with none of your context could act on it.

## An Alignment's hand-off

The hand-off is all an Alignment produces: the Project Work carries out what it lists, and nothing else of the Alignment reaches the Project. Keep it current as each part settles, so it holds even if the Owner ends the Alignment early. Use these sections:

- **Changes to make**: each change to the Vision, an Area, a Cycle or an Intent's place, stated exactly as the Owner settled it: whole texts, names, windows, judgments.
- **Why**: the Owner's reasons, briefly.
- **Not yet clear**: what the Alignment left open.
- **Ruled out**: what the Owner decided against.
- **Next for the Project Work**: the order of the changes, and the Intents to tell.

## At the other points

| Point | Reader | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| The Intent moves to In Review | the Assignee judging the handover | your final turn result: each criterion against its evidence, the Artifacts, what you could not verify |
| A child Intent is created | the child's Assignee and Work Agent | the material you hand it at creation, and Mail to its Intent: what the parent needs from it, the parent's relevant criteria and Boundary as material, never its Agreement |
