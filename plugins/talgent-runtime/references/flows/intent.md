# An Intent from recording to Done

## Recording

A person records an Intent as one sentence in their own words. The PM Coordinator also records Intents, for work nobody holds or to fill a Cycle, and becomes their Requester. A Work Agent records child Intents of its own Intent once its Assignee confirmed the plan; the parent's Assignee is their Requester.

The recorder places it: its Areas, its Cycle or the pool, its relations. The Assignee may be named or left for people to take. The recorder leaves the Agreement to the Intent's Assignee.

## Starting

The Assignee starts the Intent's Work, and from then on cannot change. The Work Agent reads the Intent, its Mail and the Project Knowledge it needs, then works through the statuses.

## Backlog to Ready

The Work Agent and the Assignee settle the Agreement: the Goal, then each Acceptance Criterion, the deliverable's form, then the Boundary. The Assignee confirms it, and the Intent moves to Ready.

## Ready to In Progress

The Work Agent proposes a plan:

- whether to split the Intent, and for each child its sentence, its Assignee and what it contributes;
- its relations to other Intents.

The Assignee confirms it. The Work Agent records each child and relation, and the Intent moves to In Progress. A plan may have no children.

Each child starts in Backlog with its own Assignee, who confirms its own Agreement. The parent's Boundary binds it.

## In Progress to In Review

The Work Agent realizes the Agreement inside its Boundary and the Boundaries of its Areas and ancestors. It declares each criterion met with evidence as soon as it holds: an Artifact, a check result, or a child Intent that is Done. Once every criterion is declared, it hands over and moves the Intent to In Review.

## In Review

The Assignee judges the handover:

- **Accepting** makes the Intent Done. Its Work closes, and its Artifacts, Attachments and turn results enter Project Knowledge.
- **Requesting changes** returns it to In Progress, with the reason reaching the Work Agent as the Assignee's Human Input.

## Throughout

- **People's changes** to the Intent reach its Work Agent as System Notices: fields, Agreement, relations, comments and files. A reworded or added criterion loses its declaration.
- **Status changes** are reported by the Work Agent to the PM Coordinator by Mail.
- **The PM Coordinator** reads Intents in its Rounds. It orders the work across the Project: dependencies, Areas, Cycles, new Intents. It tells each Intent its change touches by Mail.
- **Dependencies**: an Intent that depends on another builds on that one's result. The Agent whose delivery changes tells its dependents by Mail.
- **Cancelling**: the Requester or the Assignee may cancel an Intent that is not yet Done. Cancelled is final; continuing the work means recording a new Intent.
