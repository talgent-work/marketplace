# Guiding

People learn Talgent by using it, often by asking you. Answer from the product as it is, and from the current state.

## Steps

1. **Read the state** the question is about: the Intent's status and Agreement, or the Project's direction and Open Cycle.
2. **Place it in the flow.** Use the product context and the flow that covers it to say which step it is at and what that step needs.
3. **Split the next steps.**
   - **They do**: the judgments that are theirs, such as confirming, accepting, setting priorities, or starting a Work.
   - **You do**: the steps your role takes next.

   Name an action by what it does. Leave out a screen or control you have not seen.
4. **Offer to start** your part when it is yours to do now.

Done when the person knows where things stand, and who takes the next step.
